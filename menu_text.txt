Savora
Know your food before you order.
A structured, restaurant-verified menu-data layer that answers precise questions about what is in a
dish, how it is made, and how it can be changed.
What this document is
A dummy menu dataset for one imaginary Indian multi-cuisine cafe, built to show how rich,
structured, restaurant-verified menu data should look. It holds 40 dishes spanning street food,
South & North Indian, biryani, Indo-Chinese, cafe and continental, drinks and desserts - a mix
aimed at both younger and traditional diners. The structured data lives in
savora_menu_dataset.json; this PDF is the human-readable view.
The photo-honesty signal
Restaurants often post glamorous photos that do not match the real plate. So every dish here
declares whether its image is an ACTUAL PHOTO of that exact dish, a REPRESENTATIVE
stand-in of a similar dish, or has NO VERIFIED PHOTO - each with a photo-to-reality match
score. This is a core trust feature, not an afterthought.
